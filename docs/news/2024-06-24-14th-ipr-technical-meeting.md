# 14th IPR technical meeting Announcement

*Published: 2024-06-24*  

The next TIPR meeting will be held in **Madrid (Spain) on 14 and 15 November 2024**.

This meeting will focus on the implications of a revised Ambient Air Quality Directive, the associated reporting requirements, and the transition to ReportNet3.

The meeting will consist of talks and workshops. To facilitate productive discussions and effective collaboration, in particular during workshop sessions, **it is highly recommended attending the meeting in person**. However, for those who cannot attend in person, an online participation option will also be provided, at least for parts of the meeting.

**Further details and a formal invitation will be sent to you next autumn.**
