# 18th IPR Technical Meeting: announcement

*Published: 2026-10-06*

The 18th IPR Technical Meeting supporting the implementation of the Air Quality e-Reporting system (IPR Decision 2011/850/EU) that will take place on 4 November (8.30 to 17.00h) and 5 November (9.00 to 13.00h) at European Environment Agency, Kongens Nytorv 6, 1050 København, Denmark.

This meeting will focus on the implementation of the Ambient Air Quality Directive (EU) 2024/2881, the associated reporting requirements, and the transition to Reportnet 3, among other things. More details can be found in the Meetings section https://eeadata.github.io/AQ.Portal/pages/meetings.html
