# 12th TIPR Meeting Announcement

*Published: 2023-10-19*  
*Source: [https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2023/10/19/12th-tipr-meeting-announcement/](https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2023/10/19/12th-tipr-meeting-announcement/)*

The 12th IPR Technical Meeting supporting the implementation of the Air Quality e-Reporting system (IPR Decision 2011/850/EU) will take place on 14 November (9:00 to 12:30)  and 15 November (9:00 to 12:30)  online via MS Teams.

A detailed agenda will be available by October 20 from this AQ Portal (page: <https://eeadmz1-cws-wp-air02-dev.azurewebsites.net/aq-meetings/>).
