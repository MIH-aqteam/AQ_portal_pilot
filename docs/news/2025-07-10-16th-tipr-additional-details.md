# 16th TIPR: additional details

*Published: 2025-07-10*  
*Source: [https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2025/07/10/16th-tipr-additional-details/](https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2025/07/10/16th-tipr-additional-details/)*

The 16th TIPR (Technical Implementation and Provisions for Reporting) meeting will take place **in person on 13-14 November 2025**(one and a half day), hosted by the Institute of Environmental Protection – National Research Institute (IEP‑NRI) at Slowicza 32, Warsaw, Poland.

The primary focus of our meeting will be on the implications of the recast Ambient Air Quality Directive, the associated reporting requirements, and the transition to ReportNet3.

In connection with this, two related meetings are also scheduled at the same venue:

- **Pilot Group meeting**: 12 November (full day, in-person only)
- **Western Balkans technical session** *(tbc)*: also foreseen on 14 November (0.5 day), pending confirmation

We are very grateful to our Polish colleagues for their kind offer to host this important exchange. A formal agenda and registration details will follow in due course, but we encourage you to already **save the dates** and plan your travel accordingly.
