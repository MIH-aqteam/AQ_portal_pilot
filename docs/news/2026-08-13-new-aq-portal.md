# New AQ portal!

*Published: 2026-08-13*

The new European Air Quality Portal is now available.

The portal has been redesigned and rebuilt on a new technical platform to provide a clearer, more reliable and adaptable gateway to European air quality reporting information. Its renewed structure makes it easier to access technical documentation, reporting guidance, interactive viewers, data tables, analytical tools, meeting documents and other resources related to Air Quality eReporting.

The new platform will also allow the portal to evolve more easily as reporting requirements, tools and user needs change. Further content and improvements will be introduced progressively.

We hope you will enjoy using the new portal. Questions, comments and suggestions are welcome and may be sent to the AQ IPR Helpdesk at aqipr.helpdesk@eionet.europa.eu. If clicking the email address does not open your email application, please copy the address into a new message.
