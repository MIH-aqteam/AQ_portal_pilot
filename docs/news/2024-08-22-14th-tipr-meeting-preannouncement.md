# 14th TIPR meeting preannouncement

*Published: 2024-08-22*  

The 14th IPR Technical Meeting supporting the implementation of the Air Quality e-Reporting system (IPR Decision 2011/850/EU) will take place on 14 and 15 November  2024.

The meeting venue will be **Instituto Nacional de la Administración Pública, Atocha Street 106, 28012 Madrid, Spain**.

The preliminary time schedule is as follows:

**Day 1:** Thursday, 14 November 2024 8.30-17.00.

**Day 2:** Friday, 15 November 2024 9.00-12.30h.

The official invitation as well as the provisional agenda will follow.
