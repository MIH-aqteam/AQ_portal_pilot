# 16th TIPR: dates and location

*Published: 2025-06-05*  
*Source: [https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2025/06/05/16th-tipr-preannouncement-dates-and-location/](https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2025/06/05/16th-tipr-preannouncement-dates-and-location/)*

The 16th TIPR (Technical Implementation and Provisions for Reporting) meeting will be hosted by Poland and held in person on 13-14 November 2025 at the Institute of Environmental Protection – National Research Institute (IEP-NRI), located at Slowicza 32, Warsaw. In connection with this, the Pilot Group meeting will be held at the same venue on 12 November (in-person only).

We are grateful to our Polish colleagues for their kind offer to host the meeting, and further details, including the agenda and registration process, will follow in due course.
