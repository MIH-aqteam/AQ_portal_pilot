# Important notice: UTD data (E2a) – sFTP

*Published: 2024-04-22*  
*Source: [https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2024/04/22/important-notice-utd-data-e2a-sftp/](https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2024/04/22/important-notice-utd-data-e2a-sftp/)*

We are informing countries that, for the UTD data (E2a), the deadline for switching to sFTP is June 30th (maximum September 30th).
