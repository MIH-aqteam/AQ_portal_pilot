# 15 th TIPR meeting in Copenhagen

*Published: 2025-03-07*  
*Source: [https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2025/03/07/15th-tipr-meeting-in-copenhagen/](https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/2025/03/07/15th-tipr-meeting-in-copenhagen/)*

The meeting venue will be the European Environment Agency, Kongens Nytorv, 1050 Copenhagen, Denmark

The preliminary time schedule is as follows:

**Day 1:** Wednesday, 7 May 2025 8.30-17.00. – Followed by a possible social event (self-paid).

**Day 2:** Thursday, 8 May 2025 9.00-13:00.

As specialists in air quality e-Reporting, **your participation is crucial** for the success of this meeting, which will focus on the implications of a revised Ambient Air Quality Directive, the associated reporting requirements, and the transition to ReportNet3. These topics are essential for our continued efforts to improve and streamline air quality reporting and ensure compliance with new regulatory requirements.

The meeting will consist of presentations and discussions. To facilitate productive discussions and effective collaboration during the meeting, it is highly recommended to attend the meeting in person. However, for those who cannot attend in person, an online participation option will also be provided.

The provisional agenda and the formal invitation will be published shortly under under the section ‘[*Meetings*](../pages/meetings.md)‘ – 15th IPR technical meeting.
