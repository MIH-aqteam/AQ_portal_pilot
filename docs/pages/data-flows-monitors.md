All data flows including E1a (validated)

**Monitor All Data Flows except E2a**

Follow the data flows processing (except E2a – UTD data).

Direct link: <https://tableau-public.discomap.eea.europa.eu/views/DataMonitor_ng/General>

**Aggregations** **and continuity on E1a**

Verify the aggregations calculated by the EEA  from the E1a data flow (validated data) and the continuity of the time series over the years.

Direct link: <https://tableau-public.discomap.eea.europa.eu/views/Aggregationsandcontinuity/Thesummarydashboard>

**Aggregations** **explorer**

The objective of this tool is to replace the ‘Aggregation and Continuity on E1a’ viewer. This updated version delivers identical information but with enhanced user-friendliness. It allows to verify the aggregations calculated by the EEA  from the E1a data flow (validated data) and the continuity of the time series over the years. Additionally, it offers greater versatility, enabling users to select dimensions for presenting additional features.

Direct link: <https://tableau-public.discomap.eea.europa.eu/views/Aggregationexplorer/Dashboardaggregationstatus/>

E2a data flow (UTD data – provisional)

**UTD Submission Monitoring Dashboards (NEW: 11/06/2025)**

This viewer includes 3 dashboards for UTD:

- a general view for data submissions,
- details at country and station levels and
- EEAs processing UTD files.

**This is a new viewer which includes an upgraded version of the previous two viewers “UTD submission: general” and “UTD submission: country and station level”.**
