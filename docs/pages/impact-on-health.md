# Impact on health

*Source: [https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/impact-on-health/](https://aqportal.discomap.eea.europa.eu/impact-on-health/)*

**Health Risk Assessment**

Information on air quality Health Risk Assessments (HRA) in terms of premature deaths and  years of life lost calculated for different scenarios at:

- **Country level** – direct link: <https://discomap.eea.europa.eu/App/AQViewer/index.html?fqn=Airquality_Dissem.hra.countries_sel>

- **NUTS 3 level** – direct link: <https://discomap.eea.europa.eu/App/AQViewer/index.html?fqn=Airquality_Dissem.hra.nuts3_sel>

- **City level** – direct link: <https://discomap.eea.europa.eu/App/AQViewer/index.html?fqn=Airquality_Dissem.hra.cities_sel>
